## 3.5 Multiple heads

**Aim.** The reader knows that each attention layer runs several heads in parallel, each with its own query, key, and value matrices, and that some heads do recognizable jobs, including previous-token heads and induction heads.

**Text (draft).**

> Each attention layer contains several independent attention mechanisms, called heads. GPT-2 small has 12 heads in each of its 12 layers. Each head has its own query, key, and value matrices, so each can look for something different. The outputs of the heads are added together and written back to the model's main vector (Section 3.6).
>
> Some heads do recognizable jobs. A previous-token head attends from each position to the position just before it. An induction head carries out a copying rule: if the current token is A, and earlier in the text A was followed by B, attend to that B and predict B. An induction head works together with a previous-token head in an earlier layer, which marks each position with the token that came before it. Induction heads form suddenly during training, and their formation coincides with a jump in the model's ability to use earlier parts of its input (Olsson et al. 2022).

**Image.** Attention heat maps for three GPT-2 small heads on a random token sequence repeated twice: a previous-token head (one diagonal just below the main diagonal), an induction head (a diagonal offset by the length of the sequence), and a head with a diffuse pattern.
