# 7 Attributing beliefs and goals

Section 7 asks when, and on what evidence, it is correct to attribute beliefs and desires to an AI system. It follows Herrmann and Levinstein, Radical AI Interpretability (2026): 7.1 sets up the problem, 7.2 gives the criteria for belief, and 7.3 the criteria for desire. Section numbers in this tab refer to that book.
