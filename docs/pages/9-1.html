Open problems and how to start

Each problem below is open, matters for safety, and can be started with the ideas from this site and a single GPU or less. Each entry lists the pages that prepare you for the problem, a first project small enough for a semester, and a paper to start from. The tags for the kind of work and the setup needed are rough guides, not requirements.

Find a problem

Filter the problems by the kind of work, by the setup you have, or by the pages you have finished. A page counts as finished when you have answered its check question; finished pages are shown in blue and marked “finished”.

    Places to start

    • The ARENA curriculum: hands-on exercises in fundamentals, transformer interpretability, reinforcement learning, evaluations of language models, and alignment science, covering most of Sections 3 and 6 (learn.arena.education).
    • The TransformerLens library, which loads many open language models and lets you read and change their internal activations (github.com/TransformerLensOrg/TransformerLens).
    • The AI Alignment Forum, where much current safety research is posted and discussed (alignmentforum.org).
    • Mentorship programs: SPAR, a part-time remote research program that pairs new researchers with mentors (sparai.org), and MATS, a full-time research fellowship with mentors in Berkeley and London (matsprogram.org).

    Takeaway

    Each open problem here builds on a few pages of this site and has a first project that fits in a semester. Pick one whose pages you have finished, read the paper listed with it, and start small.
