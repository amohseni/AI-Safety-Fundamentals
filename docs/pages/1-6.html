Assistants and reasoning models

A model trained only to predict internet text continues documents. Given a question, it may answer it, or it may write more questions, because both happen in its training data. InstructGPT (2022) fine-tuned GPT-3 on example answers written by human labelers, and then on the labelers’ rankings of its own answers, a method called reinforcement learning from human feedback, or RLHF (Sections 4.2 and 4.3). Empirical finding Labelers preferred the answers of a 1.3-billion-parameter InstructGPT model to those of the 175-billion-parameter GPT-3.Ouyang, Wu, Jiang, Almeida, Wainwright, Mishkin, Zhang, Agarwal, and others (2022), “Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback,” NeurIPS; arXiv:2203.02155. ChatGPT, released in November 2022, was trained with the same method and put in a chat interface.OpenAI (2022), “Introducing ChatGPT,” November 30, 2022. The post says the model was trained with RLHF “using the same methods as InstructGPT, but with slight differences in the data collection setup.”

In the same year, Wei et al. showed that prompting a model with worked examples that write out intermediate steps, called chain-of-thought prompting, improved its accuracy on arithmetic and logic problems.Wei, Wang, Schuurmans, Bosma, Ichter, Xia, Chi, Le, and Zhou (2022), “Chain-of-thought prompting elicits reasoning in large language models,” NeurIPS; arXiv:2201.11903. Each written token is one more pass through the network, so writing out steps gives the model more computation per answer (Section 4.4).

Starting with OpenAI’s o1 (September 2024) and DeepSeek-R1 (January 2025), labs trained models with reinforcement learning on problems whose answers can be checked automatically, such as math and coding problems.OpenAI (2024), “Learning to reason with LLMs,” September 12, 2024. DeepSeek-AI (2025), “DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing reasoning capability in LLMs via reinforcement learning,” arXiv:2501.12948. These reasoning models learned to write long chains of thought before answering, to check their work, and to backtrack. In 2025, attention moved to agents: models that carry out tasks over many steps using tools.

The tool below shows real outputs from one model family, Qwen3, released by Alibaba in 2025.Yang, Li, Yang, Zhang, Hui, Zheng, Yu, Gao, and others (2025), “Qwen3 technical report,” arXiv:2505.09388. The outputs were generated once on a CPU with fixed random seeds; the models, revisions, and sampling settings are stored with them in data/1-6/outputs.json, and the script is tools/1-6/generate.py. The base model, Qwen3-0.6B-Base, was pretrained on about 36 trillion tokens and nothing else. The post-trained model, Qwen3-0.6B, started from the base model’s parameters and can run in two modes: with thinking off it answers at once, as a chat model does; with thinking on it first writes a chain of thought, marked off from its answer, as a reasoning model does. Both modes use the same parameters. One honest caveat: Qwen trained its largest models with reinforcement learning, and then trained this small model to match their outputs, so this model learned to reason from a larger reasoning model rather than through its own reinforcement learning.
